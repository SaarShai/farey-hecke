Dear Professor Koyama,

A short request, purely bibliographic.

In the continuation of our work on resonances of the Hecke groups G_q, the
next step depends on the proof mechanism of a classical result we know only
through secondary sources: Hejhal, *The Selberg Trace Formula for PSL(2,R)*,
Vol. 2, Springer Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1001 (1983), Section 7 —
specifically Theorem 7.11 and Corollary 7.12 (scattering poles accumulating
at the critical line for degenerating families; stated, with attribution to
Selberg's Göttingen lectures, in Garbin–Jorgenson, L'Enseignement Math. 64
(2018), p. 162), together with the positivity material around p. 160 that
Hejhal-school papers cite.

1. Would you be able to send a scan/PDF of §7 of Vol. 2 (roughly pp. 505–560),
   or of the two proofs if that is easier? Our institutional access does not
   cover this volume.

2. One short question, only if you happen to know off-hand: Garbin–Jorgenson
   (Thm 5.7) credit their explicit constants for the degenerating counting
   function to unpublished calculations of Hejhal. Do you know whether those
   calculations ever circulated (notes, preprint, a thesis that reproduces
   them)? A pointer would save us considerable effort; if not, no matter.

With many thanks, and best regards,

Saar
