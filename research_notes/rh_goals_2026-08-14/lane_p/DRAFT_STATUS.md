## Bibliography: resolved in v2 (2026-08-16, web-verified)

Three of these were **misattributions in v1**, not merely missing
fields. They were found by a verification pass and are the most
important v2 change after the mechanism section.

- **MMS** — v1 read "D. Mayer, T. Müller, A. Seeger, *Transfer
  operators, the Selberg zeta function and the Lewis–Zagier theory…*,
  LMS Lecture Note Ser. 397". **Wrong.** MMS is Mayer–Mühlenbruch–
  Strömberg, *The transfer operator for the Hecke triangle groups*,
  DCDS **32** (2012), no. 7, 2453–2484, arXiv:0912.2236 — which is
  also what the in-repo notes (M1F, M1D references) cite. Corrected.
- **"Borthwick–Weich 2021"** — the Lagrange–Chebyshev Schottky paper
  is by **Bandtlow, Pohl, Schick, Weiße**, Stoch. Dyn. **21** (2021),
  no. 3, art. 2140005. Key renamed `BandtlowPohlSchickWeisse21`; the
  intro sentence naming the authors was rewritten.
- **"Bruggeman–Pohl 2009 / 2023"** — v1 attributed arXiv:0912.2236 to
  Bruggeman–Pohl (it is MMS), and gave the Fredholm-determinant period
  functions paper as Bruggeman–Pohl 2023. The verified record is
  **Möller–Pohl**, ETDS **33** (2013), no. 1, 247–283, arXiv:1103.5235.
  Key `MoellerPohl13`. The intro sentence "Bruggeman–Pohl leave the
  location … conjectural" was **removed** (it was not supportable from
  the corrected records) and replaced by a weaker, defensible claim.
- **Frączek–Mayer placeholder** — resolved as far as evidence allows:
  no jointly authored Frączek–Mayer Hecke-resonance paper was located.
  Replaced by two verified items: M. S. Frączek, *Selberg Zeta
  Functions and Transfer Operators: An Experimental Approach to
  Singular Perturbations*, Springer LNM **2139** (2017); and
  Bruggeman–Frączek–Mayer, Exp. Math. **22** (2013) 217–242.
- Phillips–Sarnak Invent. Math. **80** (1985) 339–364 (DOI
  10.1007/BF01388610); Phillips–Sarnak CPAM **38** (1985) 853–866.
- Simon, Adv. Math. **24** (1977) 244–273 (DOI
  10.1016/0001-8708(77)90057-3).
- Grothendieck, Ann. Inst. Fourier **4** (1952) 73–112.
- Bandtlow–Jenkinson, ETDS **28** (2008), no. 6, 1701–1711.
- Pohl–**Paul** Wabnitz, Mem. AMS memoir no. 1616 (2026),
  arXiv:2209.05927. The v1 "volume 318" is not part of the verified
  record and was dropped.
- arXiv:2509.17936 = **K. Fedosova**; arXiv:2507.09021 =
  **Blumenthal, Nisoli, Taylor-Crush**.
- Takeuchi, JMSJ **29** (1977), no. 1, 91–106 (new bibitem, used by §5).
