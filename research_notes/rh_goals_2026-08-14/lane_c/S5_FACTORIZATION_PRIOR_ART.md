# Hecke Triangle Groups: Selberg Zeta & Transfer Operator Factorization Prior Art
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Query:** Three specific questions on factorization results for arithmetic/non-arithmetic Hecke groups.

---

## Question 1: Selberg Zeta Factorization into ζ- and Dirichlet-L Pieces (Arithmetic G₄, G₆)

**VERDICT:** PARTIAL / NOTHING-FOUND

**Findings:**

No published factorization of the form Z_G₄(s) = ζ(s) · L(s,χ) (or analogous for G₆) into separate Riemann-ζ and Dirichlet-L factors was located. This is the analogue of the classical modular-surface story for SL(2,ℤ).

**What IS published:**

- **Möller–Pohl factorization:** For any cofinite Hecke triangle group G_q (q ≥ 3, including arithmetic q ∈ {3,4,6}), the Selberg zeta function factors as:
  $$Z(s) = \det(1 - \mathcal{L}_s^+) \cdot \det(1 - \mathcal{L}_s^-)$$
  where $\mathcal{L}_s^±$ are transfer operator families arising from symbolic dynamics on the geodesic flow. The even/odd decomposition encodes even/odd Maass cusp form eigenvalues [Möller–Pohl, cited in search results; see "Eigenfunctions of Transfer Operators and Automorphic Forms for Hecke Triangle Groups of Infinite Covolume"].

- **Pohl (2014–2015):** Constructed explicit cross-sections for the geodesic flow on infinite-area Hecke triangle surfaces and demonstrated that Fredholm determinants of transfer operator families provide a factorization of the Selberg zeta function [Pohl, *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 2015, arXiv:1404.3934, "A Thermodynamic Formalism Approach to the Selberg Zeta Function for Hecke Triangle Surfaces of Infinite Area"].

**Conclusion on Q1:** The published factorization is into transfer-operator pieces (even vs. odd Maass), not into ζ × L pieces. No ζ × L analog for G₄ or G₆ is attested in the literature searched. This remains open or is not a stated research direction.

## Question 2: Transfer-Operator-Level Factorization (q=4, q=6)

**VERDICT:** PUBLISHED

**Findings:**

**Möller–Pohl (Hecke triangle groups, all q ≥ 3):**
$$Z(s) = \det(1 - \mathcal{L}_s^+) \cdot \det(1 - \mathcal{L}_s^-)$$
This factors the Selberg zeta into Fredholm determinants of two related transfer operator families, one (L_s^+) corresponding to even Maass forms, the other (L_s^−) to odd forms. Published in [Möller–Pohl; detailed in "Eigenfunctions of Transfer Operators and Automorphic Forms for Hecke Triangle Groups of Infinite Covolume", American Mathematical Society, and in arXiv:1103.5235, "Period functions for Hecke triangle groups, and the Selberg zeta function as a Fredholm determinant", *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems*, Cambridge University Press].

**Fraczek–Mayer (Γ₀(4), congruence subgroup analogue of G₄):**
Transfer operator symmetries (generated by permutation matrices P with P² = id) lead to factorizations of the Selberg zeta function in terms of Fredholm determinants of reduced transfer operators. Every symmetry induces a factorization via eigenvalue decomposition (λ = ±1 eigenspaces of the symmetry). Published in [Fraczek & Mayer, "Symmetries of the transfer operator for Γ₀(N) and a character deformation of the Selberg zeta function for Γ₀(4)", *Algebra and Number Theory* 6(3):587–610 (2012), arXiv:1011.4441].

**Conclusion on Q2:** Yes, operator-level factorizations ARE published:
- Möller–Pohl for all Hecke triangle groups (including q=4, 6)
- Fraczek–Mayer for the congruence subgroup Γ₀(4)
Both employ transfer operators and give Fredholm-determinant factorizations. These are direct extensions of Mayer's classical approach for the Gauss map / PSL(2,ℤ).

## Question 3: Non-Factorization Theorem for Non-Arithmetic Hecke Groups

**VERDICT:** NOTHING-FOUND

**Findings:**

No published statement asserting that non-arithmetic Hecke determinants admit NO factorization was located. In fact, the literature indicates the *opposite*: the factorization results by Möller–Pohl and Pohl apply uniformly to all Hecke triangle groups, whether arithmetic (q ∈ {3,4,6}) or non-arithmetic (q ∉ {3,4,6}).

The transfer operator / Fredholm determinant approach appears to be *universal* across the full family, with no special obstruction singled out for non-arithmetic cases.

**Conclusion on Q3:** Open. There is no published non-factorization theorem. The universal factorization via transfer operators suggests factorization DOES hold for non-arithmetic groups; no contrary result was found.

## Notes & Limitations

- The transfer-operator factorization (Möller–Pohl) is NOT a factorization into ζ(s) and L(s,χ) pieces. It is a factorization of Z(s) into Fredholm determinants of two commuting, related transfer operators.
- Fraczek–Mayer work on **congruence subgroups** Γ₀(N) rather than Hecke triangle groups G_q directly, but the arithmetic group Γ₀(4) is commensurable with G₄.
- The universal applicability of the transfer operator approach (both arithmetic and non-arithmetic cases) suggests any "obstruction to factorization" for non-arithmetic groups is not a known theorem.
- A classical analogue (ζ × L factorization for modular surfaces) exists, but the literature does not establish an analogue for arithmetic Hecke triangle groups G₄, G₆.
