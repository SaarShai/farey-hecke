## I. V5-N1 — covariance envelope passes; full inverse envelope does not

### I.1 The displayed covariance/Fisher Loewner envelope is proved

Let (D_x(\omega)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}x_ke^{i\omega k\Delta}).  Since
((\mathrm{M\text{-}n})) gives (\Omega\Delta\le\pi/2<\pi), Parseval on the
Nyquist cell gives

\[
\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\pi/\Delta}^{\pi/\Delta}|D_x(\omega)|^2\,d\omega
=\Delta^{-1}\|x\|^2.
\]

Therefore, with (0\le S_\eta\le S_{\max}),

\[
x^{\mathsf T}\Sigma_\eta x
\le \frac{S_{\max}}{\Delta}\|x\|^2,
\qquad
\sigma_s^2I\preceq\Sigma\preceq
(\sigma_s^2+S_{\max}/\Delta)I.
\]

Loewner inversion reverses these inequalities.  Congruence by the sampled
derivative matrix (D), with (G^{\mathrm{disc}}=D^{\mathsf T}D), gives exactly

\[
\frac{\Delta}{S_s+S_{\max}}G^{\mathrm{disc}}
\preceq I^{(n)}\preceq
\frac{\Delta}{S_s}G^{\mathrm{disc}},
\qquad S_s=\sigma_s^2\Delta.
\]

Thus Lemma 5 and (A\(\infty\).0), v6 lines 558--594, are correct.  No Szegő
step is needed.

The exact numerical maximum is

\[
L_1=\log(\gamma_1/2\pi)=0.810757479321444676\ldots,
\qquad
S_{\max}=\frac{16}{9}L_1=1.441346629904790535\ldots .
\]

Hence (S_{\max}\le1.4414) is a safe **UP** bound.  V6's intermediate
(L_1=0.81075727) and (S_{\max}=1.4413462578) are inaccurate, but the final
ceiling has (5.33\times10^{-5}) headroom.

### I.2 The right side of (A\(\infty\).1) is not proved

Lemma 2 defines (H) as the ((\omega,\phi)) principal block of
(G^{\mathrm{disc}}/A^2) (v6 lines 416--424).  Equation (5.5) therefore
upper-bounds ([H^{-1}]_{\omega\omega}), not the inverse entry of the full
free-amplitude (3\times3) Gram.

Partition the latter as

\[
G_j=
\begin{pmatrix}
q_0&r^{\mathsf T}\\
r&H
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Generically (r\ne0): its components contain
(-\frac A2\sum t_k\sin(2(\omega t_k+\phi))) and
(-\frac A2\sum\sin(2(\omega t_k+\phi))).  The lower-right block of the
full inverse is

\[
(H-r r^{\mathsf T}/q_0)^{-1}\succeq H^{-1}.
\]

V6 line 433 itself uses this direction to justify a **lower** bound after the
amplitude row is added.  It cannot turn an upper bound for (H^{-1}) into an
upper bound for (G_j^{-1}).  Nevertheless line 696 does exactly that when it
uses (5.5) for the right side of (A\(\infty\).1).

The lower side of (A\(\infty\).1) is valid.  The upper side needs a new full
(3\times3) amplitude-Schur estimate.  Under v6's stated weakening policy, the
minimal repair is to delete that upper side and the resulting “two-sided” claim.

## II. V5-N2 — tied score passes; Theorem B has a new support defect

### II.1 Independent tied-score derivation

For

\[
u(\omega)=(\omega^2+\tfrac14)(\omega^2+\tfrac94)
=\omega^4+\tfrac52\omega^2+\tfrac9{16},
\qquad a=u^{-1/2},
\]

one has

\[
u'=4\omega(\omega^2+\tfrac54),
\qquad
a'=-2\omega(\omega^2+\tfrac54)u^{-3/2},
\qquad
A'=2a'=-4\omega(\omega^2+\tfrac54)u^{-3/2}.
\]

Thus the tied derivative and its Fisher information are

\[
\frac{dm}{d\gamma_j}=\partial_{\gamma_j}m+A'(\gamma_j)\partial_{A_j}m,
\]

\[
I_{\mathrm{tied},j}
=I_{\gamma_j\gamma_j}^{\mathrm{free}}
+2A'_jI_{\gamma_jA_j}^{\mathrm{free}}
+(A'_j)^2I_{A_jA_j}^{\mathrm{free}}.
\]

The appearances of (I^{\mathrm{free}}) in (8.4) are only this coordinate
chain-rule identity.  V6 line 795 expressly distinguishes it from the
free-amplitude efficient Fisher.  Theorem B's (B.0) uses
(\mathbb E[I_{\mathrm{tied},j}]); the locally-white skeleton is repeatedly
disclaimed as that expectation.  No residual substitution of the free-amplitude
efficient Fisher was found.

At (j=10), (n=1124), the independent values are

\[
\left|\frac{A'}A\right|=0.0401614990506882\ldots<
\frac2{\gamma_{10}}=0.0401817561646909\ldots .
\]

Using the exact ratios in (8.5) gives

\[
\delta_{10}^{\mathrm{tied}}<4.2042658291\times10^{-5};
\]

using the exact (A'/A) gives (4.2013218072\times10^{-5}).  Even the
coarser displayed coefficient (30.878) gives
(4.2048138575\times10^{-5}).  Therefore

\[
\delta_{10}^{\mathrm{tied}}\le4.21\times10^{-5}
\]

is safely rounded **UP**.

### II.2 Fresh blocker: the product prior is not supported in (M5)

Theorem B line 858 imports Theorem A's hypotheses, including (M5): all tone
gaps and the cut margin must be at least (h=2\pi K/T).  But (P1\(\prime\prime\))
uses independent intervals of half-width (\alpha=h/2).

At the operating point,

\[
h=1.459787859377701659\ldots,
\qquad
\alpha=0.729893929688850830\ldots .
\]

For the tenth centre,

\[
\Gamma_{\mathrm{op}}-\mu_{10}=h+2.94999634\times10^{-9}.
\]

At the upper end of its prior support the cut margin is only

\[
\Gamma_{\mathrm{op}}-(\mu_{10}+\alpha)
=0.72989393263884717\ldots<h.
\]

The violation is not confined to a zero-density endpoint: approximately half
the raised-cosine prior mass lies above the (M5)-admissible cut threshold.
Likewise, if two centres have gap (g<2h), independent radius-(h/2) draws
can have gap below (h).  V6 itself lists (g_{10,9}=1.7686815965\ldots<2h).

Thus the product prior is not contained in the parameter region whose
regularity Theorem B imports.  One must either remove (M5) from Theorem B and
prove the needed rank/regularity directly on the prior box, or change the
prior/cut and recompute its information.  Neither is done or ledgered.

### II.3 Fresh floor/cancellation defect at (j=1)

\[

\[
