## Executive ruling

**VERDICT: NOT-PROMOTABLE.**

The rewrite is materially cleaner and more honest than v3. It makes a coherent deterministic-mark Gaussian-surrogate choice, deletes the Godambe/score-class claim, separates pointwise and Bayes risks, disclaims transfer to the phase sum, carries the Proposition R assumptions, and replaces the superseded overlay by an integrated text.

It does **not** satisfy all seven minimum conditions. Of the 19 prior defects, this review rules **11 REPAIRED, 3 CARRIED-AS-OPEN-HONESTLY, and 5 STILL-DEFECTIVE**. More importantly:

1. the exact band-limited continuous Gaussian experiment is not regular as claimed: the pure-tone mean shifts are not in the RKHS of a compactly band-limited stationary process, so §9 (R1) and the exact Fisher formula (4.0) do not follow;
2. Theorem B mixes Theorem A's pointwise amplitude relation with an independent product prior over amplitudes and ordinates, while leaving the needed averaged Fisher/nuisance calculation OWED;
3. the declared cut is below the M5 threshold for the full stored tenth ordinate;
4. the GAP-4 and finite-window constants used in Theorem A remain unproved; and
5. the advertised `0.0419` is arithmetically downward-rounded but is not a certified lower bound after two omitted factors.

The operating-point arithmetic mostly passes. The exceptions are a wrong asymptotic variance, misuse of a variance ratio as an RMSE ratio, unsafe upward rounding of the sample-complexity lower-bound coefficient, and several inaccurate intermediate values.

## III. New defects exposed by the rewrite

### BLOCKING

#### N1. Compact band limitation makes the asserted Gaussian experiment singular, not regular

**Location:** §§2.1, 2.2 `(M4′)`, §4, §9 `(R1)`, and (4.0).

The noise is stipulated to have spectral support `|ν|≤Ω`, while the mean on `[0,T]` is an exact pure-tone polynomial. The RKHS of a stationary process with compact spectral support consists of restrictions of band-limited functions. If such a function equals `cos(γt+φ)` on a nonempty interval, analytic continuation forces equality on the whole line; its spectral transform is then atomic, not an `L^2(S_η^{-1}dν)` Cameron–Martin element. Consequently the pure-tone mean shift is not in the RKHS, and the Gaussian laws are mutually singular rather than mutually absolutely continuous.

Restricting the Fourier transform of the **zero-extended time-limited derivative** to `|ν|≤Ω` does not repair this: that projection no longer equals the original derivative on `[0,T]`. Thus (4.0) is not the exact Fisher information of the continuous record declared in §2.1. v4 would need a different observation model—e.g. an explicitly projected finite-energy mean in an abstract Gaussian Hilbert experiment, or discrete/noisy sampling—and a fresh regularity/Fisher derivation.

#### N2. Theorem B's parameter/prior quantifier is internally inconsistent

**Location:** `(M-amp)` at lines 167–169, prior at lines 510–524, averaged-information paragraph at lines 526–537, and Theorem B at lines 539–565.

Theorem B says to assume Theorem A's hypotheses, which include the pointwise true-parameter relation `A_j=2a(γ_j)`, but then puts an independent raised-cosine prior on `A_j` and `γ_j`. Almost every prior draw violates that relation. If `(M-amp)` is dropped under the prior, the cancellation giving `I_eff=T^3/(6 log(γ/2π))` is lost and `E_π[A_j^2/S_η(γ_j)]` must be computed. If it is retained, the prior cannot be the stated product prior.

The factors `F_flat`, `F_leak`, and `F_win` also vary over the prior support, but (B.1) pulls them outside `E_π[I]` without a uniform support bound. `OWED-B-avg` and `OWED-amp-avg` are therefore load-bearing gaps, not optional refinements.

#### N3. The replacement operating point fails M5 for the actual stored target

**Location:** §§11.1–11.2 and claims list line 981.

The full `γ_10` used in the table and as the external prior centre gives a threshold `51.2336203370500`, above `Γ_op`. A theorem-operating cut cannot switch to the six-decimal truncation solely to pass a strict hypothesis. The minimal eight-decimal repair is `Γ_op=51.23362034`.

### MAJOR

#### N4. The “partially certified floor” is not a lower bound

**Location:** §12 lines 816–824.

From (A.3), the would-be lower bound contains `sqrt(F_flat F_leak F_cross F_win)`. Omitting factors in `[0,1]` raises the right-hand side. The resulting `0.0419` is therefore not a weaker certified floor; it is an upper envelope on a still-unknown lower-bound expression. Moreover `F_flat` itself is modulo an OWED remainder and `κ_d` lacks the promised receipt.

#### N5. The full-parameter CR proof changes `I` from `3d×3d` to `3×3` without the required matrix step

**Location:** §3 `(B1)_j`, Lemma 3′, and Theorem A proof line 475.

The theorem estimates the full `3d`-parameter vector, but `(B1)_j` is defined after treating the other tones as fixed. A valid weaker per-tone lower bound can be obtained by explicitly applying the nuisance Schur-complement inequality to the full Fisher matrix, then bounding the diagonal `3×3` block. That step is absent. Lemma 3′ instead inserts an unproved `F_cross`; its displayed block-norm hypothesis is not the symmetrically normalised block bound normally needed for a Loewner perturbation argument.

#### N6. Lemma 1″ states an invalid componentwise matrix inequality

**Location:** lines 319–331.

Pointwise spectral bounds imply a **Loewner-order** Gram inequality

\[
(1+\delta_j)^{-1}S_\eta(\gamma_j)^{-1}G_j^P
\preceq I_{N_j}\preceq
(1+\delta_j)S_\eta(\gamma_j)^{-1}G_j^P,
\]

not the entrywise inequalities written for every `αβ`; cross-Gram integrands have no fixed sign. The subsequent inversion argument needs the Loewner statement.

#### N7. The current ledger is not one-row-per-open-item

See §V below. This directly defeats v4's own ledger contract and the user's requested check.

### MINOR

1. `σ_as^2` and `σ_as` are numerically wrong; the appended explanation understates the relative discrepancy by a factor of ten.
2. §12 applies a variance ratio directly to RMSE instead of taking its square root.
3. `C_X=2.3157` is rounded in the unsafe direction for a sample-complexity lower bound.
4. The literal claim that the phrase “uniformly in `d`” does not occur is false: it occurs in a negated sentence at Theorem A. The mathematical claim remains non-uniform, so this is wording only.
5. Several intermediate equalities (`Γ/(2π)`, `L`, `Γ(L+1/3)`, and `H_9/π`) are not accurate to their displayed digits.

6. correct the numerical and rounding errors; and
