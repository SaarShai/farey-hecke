## II. N1: exact discrete experiment versus Proposition A\(\infty\)

### Finite-dimensional experiment

The declared observation is coherent.  For fixed \(n\),

\[
Y\sim N(m(\theta),\Sigma),\qquad
\Sigma=\Sigma_\eta+\sigma_s^2I_n\succeq\sigma_s^2I_n\succ0.
\]

The mean-coordinate derivatives are

\[
\partial_{A_j}m_k=\cos(\gamma_jt_k+\phi_j),\quad
\partial_{\phi_j}m_k=-A_j\sin(\gamma_jt_k+\phi_j),\quad
\partial_{\gamma_j}m_k=-A_jt_k\sin(\gamma_jt_k+\phi_j).
\]

Because \(\Sigma\) is fixed in \(\theta\), (4.0n)
\(I_{\alpha\beta}=(\partial_\alpha m)^T\Sigma^{-1}
(\partial_\beta m)\) is exactly the Fisher information.  The positive sampling
noise removes the v4 Cameron–Martin singularity.  The sampling design also has
ample dimension: at the operating point \(n_\star=1124>3d=30\), and its
Nyquist frequency is above the whole declared covariance band.

The claim at line 693 that “(M5) plus \(A_j>0\)” alone proves nonsingularity is
too compressed—rank also uses the sampling design and absence of discrete
aliasing—but the actual operating design supplies those missing facts.

### Why Proposition A\(\infty\) fails as stated

Equation (A\(\infty\).1) is an equality.  Its factors are not exact
decompositions:

1. \(F^{\rm flat}\) comes from a Loewner sandwich, not an identity.
2. \(F^{\rm win,\infty}=1-32/(\gamma T)\) is a lower comparison envelope,
   not the exact continuous-window ratio (which also depends on phase).
3. \(F^{\rm leak}\) is defined from a one-sided eigenvalue bound.  Setting it
   to 1 when (B1) is not assumed cannot make an equality true.
4. Lemma 3′ gives only
   \([I^{-1}]_{jj}\succeq I_{jj}^{-1}\); cross-tone nuisance effects do not
   disappear as \(n\to\infty\) at fixed \(T,d\).

None of these discrepancies is included in
\(|E_n|\le E_{\rm alias}+E_{\rm disc}+E_{\rm Sz}\).  Moreover the covariance
symbol is cut to zero outside \([-\Omega,\Omega]\) while
\(S_\eta(\Omega)>0\), so the band edge is discontinuous; the proof's stated
“\(C^1\) symbol” Szegő premise is false.  The symbol also changes with \(n\)
under the fixed-\(S_s\) scaling.  `OWED-Szego` cannot turn the displayed
equality into a proved proposition.

Aliasing itself is not the defect: \(n\ge n_\star\) gives
\(\pi/\Delta\ge2\Omega\), so the supported tail spectrum has no replicas in
the principal cell.  The problem is the unsupported inverse-Toeplitz and
equality passage after that observation.

The sampling-noise role is honestly visible in Theorem A.  It changes the
finite theorem's constant to
\(24\sigma_s^2/(A_j^2nT^2)\); therefore the old coloured \(T^{-3}\) constant
is not a finite-\(n\) consequence.  V5 says this correctly.  In A\(\infty\),
however, holding \(S_s=\sigma_s^2T/n\) fixed forces
\(\sigma_s^2=S_sn/T\), and recovery of \(6\log(\gamma/2\pi)\) needs the
additional \(S_s/S_\eta(\gamma)\to0\) limit.  That double-limit caveat is
stated, but the proposition preceding it remains defective.

## III. N2: prior, averaged Fisher, and monotonicity

The following calculus directions in §8.3 are correct:

- \(T^3/[6\log(\gamma/2\pi)]\) is strictly decreasing for \(\gamma>2\pi\),
  so its prior average is at most its value at \(\mu_j-\alpha\).
- Since Lemma 4 gives \(D<0<D'\),
  \(F^{\rm flat}(\gamma)=\exp(2hD(\gamma-h))\) is increasing in \(\gamma\).
  Its support infimum is therefore at \(\mu_j-\alpha-h\), as printed.
- \(F^{\rm win}(\gamma)=1-84/(\gamma T)\) is increasing, so its infimum is
  at \(\mu_j-\alpha\).

The failure is upstream of those endpoints.  Under the ordinate-only model
\(A_j=A_j(\gamma_j)\), the total derivative of the mean is

\[
\frac{d m}{d\gamma_j}
=\partial_{\gamma_j}m+A_j'(\gamma_j)\partial_{A_j}m.
\]

The efficient information obtained by treating \(A_j\) as a free nuisance
coordinate projects out the \(\partial_{A_j}m\) direction.  It is not the
information of the tied-amplitude submodel.  Hence lines 620 and 654 cannot
alternate between “the exact discrete \(I_{jj}\) as a function of \(\gamma\)”
and the tied path.  A fresh Fisher matrix for ordinates and phases, followed by
the applicable multivariate/circular-prior van Trees inequality, is required.
The open `OWED-H-circle` is also load-bearing for the theorem as written, not
an optional refinement after the theorem.

## V. Window constants

**\(C_{\rm win}=84\): sustained.**  From the displayed discrete Abel bounds,

\[
r(\tau)=\frac{1-\pi/(2\tau)}
{1+26\pi/\tau+6\pi^2/\tau^2},\qquad
\tau(1-r(\tau))
=\frac{53\pi/2+6\pi^2/\tau}
{1+26\pi/\tau+6\pi^2/\tau^2}.
\]

For \(\tau\ge218.22\), this is below 83.53, so 84 is safe.  The determinant
direction is also correct: upper-bounding \(ac-b^2\) and lower-bounding the
numerator \(c\) gives a lower bound on the inverse \(\omega\omega\) entry.

**\(C_{\rm win}^{\infty}=32\): sustained, but poorly receipted.**  Applying
the same algebra to the three continuous remainder bounds printed at lines
454–463 gives, with \(q=\omega T\),
\(\varepsilon_a=3/q+3/q^2+3/(2q^3)\),
\(\varepsilon_b=2/q+1/q^2\), and \(\varepsilon_c=1/q\).
The resulting \(q(1-r(q))\) is below 29 on \(q\ge218.22\) (for example
25.81 at the left endpoint and 28.56 at \(\omega=\Omega\)); hence 32 is safe.
The v5 prose instead quotes 25.77, 31.75, and 29.27 without displaying the
formula that produces them.  Those figures should be deleted or receipted,
but they do not make the looser constant 32 false.

## VIII. New defects

### BLOCKING

#### V5-N1. Proposition A\(\infty\) promotes comparison inequalities to an equality

**Location:** lines 539–579, especially (A\(\infty\).1)–(A\(\infty\).2).

The displayed factors are bounds, not multiplicative identities, and the
named error does not cover their slack or cross-tone nuisance information.
The proposition and its limiting equality do not follow.

#### V5-N2. Theorem B uses the free-amplitude Fisher after tying amplitude to ordinate

**Location:** lines 614–620 and 644–675.

The score of the tied submodel contains \(A_j'(\gamma_j)\partial_{A_j}m\).
The \(3d\)-coordinate efficient information projects that direction out as a
free nuisance.  It is not the Fisher information entering van Trees for the
declared prior.  Consequently (B.0)'s identification and (B.1)'s advertised
repair do not prove Theorem B.

### MAJOR

#### V5-N3. The Szegő premise does not match the declared symbol

**Location:** lines 564 and 577.

The band-limited \(S_\eta\) is abruptly zero beyond \(\Omega\) while positive
at the edge, so the symbol is not \(C^1\).  It also changes with \(n\) under
the fixed-\(S_s\) scaling.  A standard fixed-symbol \(C^1\) Toeplitz remainder
cannot simply be named and assumed.

#### V5-N4. Circular/nuisance prior regularity is left OWED inside a claimed theorem

**Location:** lines 620 and 644–675.

Theorem B invokes a scalar van Trees display while phases and other ordinates
remain nuisance/random coordinates and `OWED-H-circle` is open.  This is a
load-bearing theorem hypothesis, not merely a choice between two equivalent
presentations.

### MINOR

1. The Lemma-4 numerical table has unsafe directions at \(j=1\) and \(j=2\).
2. The continuous-window receipt values at line 465 do not follow from the
   “identical envelope” immediately displayed; the constant 32 remains safe.
3. \(L_{10}\) and \(\sqrt{6L_{10}}\) have small last-digit inaccuracies.
4. Line 693 attributes full Fisher nonsingularity to M5 and \(A_j>0\) without
