**Honesty constraints carried over from the survey.** Do not pitch this as a refutation of arithmetic programs (Connes, Eisenstein/Maass–Selberg, RMT, de Bruijn–Newman). Those are class (a): arithmetic is load-bearing. The no-go disciplines generic Hilbert–Pólya / Berry–Keating / PT-symmetry schemas (class (b)).
