# N(18) feasibility pilot — maximum equiangular lines in R^18

Date: 2026-06-14. Status of the problem: **57 ≤ N(18) ≤ 59, open** (smallest open
dimension of the Haantjes 1948 / Lemmens-Seidel 1973 problem).

This is a feasibility study (concrete progress + honest difficulty bound), not a
claim to have closed the problem.

---

## Step 0 — Literature (grounded, cited)

The exact reduction (the object that makes any resolution self-certifying), from
Greaves–Syatriadi–Yatsyna, *Equiangular lines in Euclidean spaces: dimensions 17
and 18*, Math. Comp. 92 (2023) 1867–1903, arXiv:2104.04330, §1:

> Let L be an equiangular line system of cardinality n in R^d with n>d, spanned
> by unit vectors v_1..v_n. The off-diagonal inner products are ±α, α∈(0,1). The
> Gram matrix G has 1 on the diagonal and ±α off-diagonal. **S = (G−I)/α** is the
> Seidel matrix: symmetric, 0 diagonal, ±1 off-diagonal. Since G is PSD with rank
> ≤ d, **the smallest eigenvalue of S is −1/α with multiplicity at least n−d.**

For the relevant common angle in this range, **α = 1/5** (smallest Seidel
eigenvalue −5):

  *n equiangular lines in R^d at arccos(1/5)  ⟺  ∃ Seidel matrix S of order n
  with  λ_min(S) ≥ −5  and  mult(−5 in spec S) ≥ n−d.*
  Equivalently G = I + (1/5)S is PSD of rank = n − mult(−5) ≤ d.

Bounds and their sources:
- **N(17) = 48** — settled, Greaves–Syatriadi–Yatsyna 2021 (arXiv:2104.04330).
- **N(18) ≥ 57** — same paper, §2, explicit construction (matrices F1..F4 of 57
  integer vectors in Z^18 of squared-norm 10; e.g. char S1 =
  (x+5)^39(x−4)(x−7)(x−9)^2(x−11)^9(x−13)^4(x−15)).  Previous record was 56
  ("searching inside the Witt design"); their construction uses norm-10 integer
  vectors with pairwise inner product ±2.
- **N(18) ≤ 59** — Greaves–Syatriadi, *Real equiangular lines in dimension 18 and
  the Jacobi identity for complementary subgraphs*, Lin. Alg. Appl. (2023),
  arXiv:2206.04267, Thm 1.1.  (The 2021 paper had ≤ 60; the relative bound is
  ≤ 61 = ⌊18·24/7⌋ = ⌊432/7⌋.)
- 2025 work (arXiv:2503.06377, A9⊕A9⊕A1) reconfirms 57 ≤ N(18) ≤ 59 and proves
  the 57-systems are *strongly maximal* (not extendable, even in higher dim).
- Asymptotic / large-d resolution: Jiang–Tidor–Yao–Zhang–Zhao, Annals 2021
  (spectral-radius order), settles d large; d=18 is below its range.

## Step 1 — Oracle (exact-arithmetic framework, validated)

Code: `projects/N18_pilot/code/eqlines.py`, `verify_system.py`.
All certificates are exact (integer Seidel matrix → integer characteristic
polynomial; multiplicity by exact polynomial division; λ_min ≥ −5 by an exact
Sturm real-root count strictly below −5; rank cross-checked mod a large prime).

**Oracle A (settled value, integer-exact).** Petersen two-graph Seidel matrix S
on 10 vertices: char S = (x−3)^5 (x+3)^5, λ_min = −3 with multiplicity 5 ⇒
G = I + (1/3)S is PSD of rank 10−5 = 5. Certifies a realization of **10
equiangular lines in R^5 at arccos(1/3)**, and N(5)=10 is the settled maximum.
The exact engine returns mult=5, rank=5 — PASS.

**Oracle B (the actual 1/5 reduction).** A regenerated **56**-line system in R^18
(the pre-2021 record) is certified exactly: char poly (x+5)^38 (x−9)(x−11)^8
(x−13)(deg-8), λ_min=−5, mult(−5)=38 = 56−18, rank G = 18 ≤ 18 — PASS.

Both oracles pass. Edge-case tests of the exact λ_min routine (Petersen at
bounds −2/−3/−4, K3 Seidel) all correct.

## Step 2a — Witness search (lower bound)

Recipe reproduced verbatim from arXiv:2104.04330 §2:
- L0 = { v ∈ Z^18 : ⟨v,v⟩ = 10 }.  Generator validated: it produces 73,617,480
  vectors = 2 × 36,808,740 (the paper's figure counts lines, i.e. up to sign);
  analytic per-pattern count {[3,1],[2,2,1,1],[2,1^6],[1^10]} matches exactly.
- Pick v1; intersect to vectors with ⟨v,vi⟩ = ±2; grow a basis of 18 and a
  clique. norm 10, inner products ±2 ⇒ angle α = 2/10 = 1/5.
- The whole n-line system is one clique in the "±2" graph on norm-10 vectors.

Search infrastructure: `search57.py`, `search58.py`, `maxclique.py`; run in
parallel across M1 (192.168.1.22, 10 cores), M2 (192.168.1.92, 12 cores), and
the local Mac.

**RESULT (reproduced N(18) ≥ 57).** Two independent 57-line systems found and
**exactly certified**:
- `sys_seed304_n57.json`: char (x+5)^39 (x−7)(x−9)(x−11)^7(x−13)^2(x−15)(x²−22x+113)
  (x⁴−39x³+551x²−3329x+7264); mult(−5)=39=57−18, rank 18 — PASS.
- `sys_seed303_n57.json`: char (x+5)^39 (x−11)^9 (x−13)(deg-8); mult(−5)=39,
  rank 18 — PASS.  (Same −5-multiplicity-39 family as the published S1.)
Independently cross-checked in float: all norms 10, off-diag Gram ∈ {−2,+2},
Seidel λ_min = −5 (mult 39), normalized Gram PSD of rank 18.

**Maximality of the reproduced 57-systems (exact).** Scanning all 73,617,480
norm-10 integer vectors, the system `sys_seed304_n57.json` has **0** vectors that
are ±2 with all 57 of its members — i.e. it cannot be extended to 58 lines within
the norm-10/±2 lattice family.  (`ext_scan.py`.)  This independently confirms,
for our reproduced object, the strong-maximality result of arXiv:2503.06377.  A
58-line witness therefore cannot be obtained by extending a 57-system in this
family; it would require a genuinely different two-graph / construction.

**58-witness search.** Parallel tabu/Bron–Kerbosch max-clique search over many
random anchor vectors v1 (M1 10c + M2 12c + local 6c, ~3000 s each, ~28 workers),
seeking a 40-clique among the survivors (⇒ 58 lines).  The search repeatedly tops
out at exactly **57** (clique 39) — the same ceiling as the published systems and
consistent with the family being maximal at 57.  No 58-line system was found.
This is expected: the entire search space here is the norm-10/±2 clique family
that is now proven to be maximal at 57; a 58 (if it exists) lives outside it.

## Step 2b — Upper-bound certificate (reach assessment)

Exact bounds reproduced: relative bound N(18) ≤ ⌊18·(25−1)/(25−18)⌋ = ⌊432/7⌋ =
**61**; Gerzon absolute d(d+1)/2 = 171.  The hard improvements 61→60→59 are full
research papers.

To certify **N(18) ≤ 58** one must show **no order-59 Seidel matrix** has
λ_min ≥ −5 and mult(−5) ≥ 41.  Greaves–Syatriadi (2022) proved ≤ 59 by ruling out
order-**60** (mult ≥ 42): enumerate all candidate characteristic polynomials (44
of them), kill 43 by modular/interlacing arguments, kill the last by a
strongly-regular-graph nonexistence theorem.  The same paper states explicitly
that enumerating the candidate char-polys for order **59** "may be possible on a
supercomputer but appears to be out of reach" on a PC.  Direct Seidel enumeration
is hopeless (order-59 two-graphs ≈ 2^1653 ≈ 10^498).  So a PC-scale rational
Farkas/SDP-dual certificate for ≤ 58 is **not** in reach here.

We did reproduce the elementary necessary conditions that gate the candidate
enumeration (the cheap part). For an order-n Seidel matrix with char poly
(x+5)^m·g(x), m=n−18, deg g = 18: tr S = 0 and tr S² = n(n−1) force the 18
"other" eigenvalues (roots of g) to have power sums p1 = Σμ = 5m and
p2 = Σμ² = n(n−1) − 25m. For the open orders:
  n=58 (m=40): (p1,p2) = (200, 2306);  n=59 (m=41): (205, 2397).
The Cauchy–Schwarz bound p2 ≥ p1²/18 is **satisfied** in every open case
(57,58,59,60), so none is excluded by power-sum constraints — confirming that
ruling out 58/59 needs the full degree-18 integer-polynomial enumeration under
the deeper modular (mod-2^e) + interlacing + SRG-nonexistence machinery, which is
exactly the supercomputer-scale step.

## Verdict (calibrated)

**Oracle: PASS** (both the settled-value oracle N(5)=10 via Petersen and the
genuine 1/5 reduction via an exactly-certified 56-line R^18 system).

**Lower bound: N(18) ≥ 57 reproduced and exactly certified** (two independent
witnesses, `sys_seed303/304_n57.json`, λ_min=−5, mult 39 = 57−18, rank 18; cross-
checked in float). This matches but does not exceed the published lower bound.

**No 58/59 witness found.** The reproduced 57-systems are exactly maximal within
the norm-10/±2 lattice family (0 extenders out of 73.6M candidates), and a broad
parallel max-clique search over that family tops out at exactly 57. A 58-line
witness, if it exists, lies outside this family and would need a different
construction (different two-graph / lattice). We found no such object.

**No upper-bound certificate produced.** Reachable exact bounds (relative ≤ 61,
Gerzon ≤ 171) are far above 59; SDP/pillar/k-point bounds reach only ≤ 59 in the
literature. Improving to ≤ 58 requires the order-59 candidate-char-poly
enumeration that the authors of the ≤ 59 proof explicitly flag as supercomputer-
scale, plus per-candidate strongly-regular-graph nonexistence arguments.

**Reachability of FULL resolution of N(18):** NOT in our compute reach.
- *Lower-bound side (proving N(18) ≥ 58):* would require discovering a 58-line
  two-graph outside the (now-known-maximal-at-57) norm-10 family. There is no
  known structured candidate; this is open research with no evident handle.
  Calibration: **unknown / needs new structure**, not days-or-weeks of search.
- *Upper-bound side (proving N(18) ≤ 58):* requires the order-59 enumeration the
  experts call supercomputer-scale, plus new SRG-nonexistence results. Calibration:
  **weeks-to-months on a cluster at best, possibly needs new theory** — not a PC
  task, and well beyond this fleet (M1 10c + M2 12c + 1 Mac).

The honest bottom line: this pilot **validated the exact self-certifying
machinery end-to-end** and **independently reproduced N(18) ≥ 57**, but moving
either bound is a genuine research-scale undertaking, not a compute-budget away.
The most concrete new (if modest) exactly-verified fact produced here is the
maximality witness: a specific certified 57-system with provably zero norm-10/±2
extensions.
