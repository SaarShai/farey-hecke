## Aim 1 — X_Ω as a new arithmeticity/commensurability invariant + quantitative Veech dichotomy
**Target.** Prove the slope-gap support edge X_Ω is a genuine geometric invariant of a lattice surface / Veech group that *distinguishes arithmetic from non-arithmetic*, with an exact computable value — a Veech-dichotomy refinement in the gap category.
**Already have.** Exact `X_Ω = 1/λ³` for Hecke (machine-verified lower bound); `B=2 ⟺ {3,4,6}` (= arithmetic Hecke groups, Takeuchi ∘ cluster-ceiling); the uniform horocycle-section method; cluster-ceiling growth law.
**Steps.**
1. Close the `X_Ω = 1/λ³` **equality** (currently only the lower bound is machine-verified; the upper bound needs the genuine-engine cusp-Dirac argument — scoped as G-D / G-F).
2. Compute X_Ω for a **second family** (Bouw–Möller surfaces / a Teichmüller curve) to test invariance + the dichotomy.
3. Prove the dichotomy (arithmetic ⟺ X_Ω in a distinguished set).
4. Machine-verify.
**Closeness:** MEDIUM. **Significance:** HIGH (new computable invariant; generalizes the Sarnak/Koyama arithmeticity-detection paradigm into a fresh category).
**Novelty gate:** vs Athreya–Cheung, Uyanik–Work (Veech gap distributions), quantitative Veech dichotomy literature.

## Aim 2 — Certified effective equidistribution of the Hecke/Rosen-CF horocycle (explicit constants)
**Target.** An effective equidistribution / gap-distribution theorem for the BCZ–Farey horocycle on Hecke surfaces with **explicit, certified rate and constants**, via the Rosen-CF Gauss-map transfer-operator spectral gap.
**Already have.** `M` = the Rosen-CF transfer matrix (established in-project); transfer-operator + interval-arithmetic machinery; the gap-distribution support edge.
**Steps.**

2. Certify its spectral gap (Lagrange–Chebyshev / interval arithmetic, à la Pollicott–Vytnova).
3. Convert gap → effective equidistribution rate with explicit constant.
4. Machine-verify the reduction.
**Closeness:** HIGH (assembly + certification; ingredients exist). **Significance:** MED–HIGH (effective equidistribution is a hot frontier; explicit constants are what abstract proofs lack). **This is the analytic engine for Aim 1.**

## Aim 3 — Quantum chaos for non-arithmetic Hecke: certified spectra → QUE test
**Target.** Produce the first *rigorous* (interval-certified) Laplacian eigenvalues / Selberg-zeta zeros for non-arithmetic Hecke surfaces, then test the QUE-analog and level-spacing-vs-arithmeticity — potentially a **certified anomaly** where arithmetic QUE (Lindenstrauss) does not extend.

**Steps.**

**Closeness:** LOW–MEDIUM (spectral data reachable; a QUE theorem is hard). **Significance:** VERY HIGH (quantum chaos / QUE is one of the widest-audience arenas).
**Novelty gate:** vs arithmetic-QUE literature (Lindenstrauss, Soundararajan), Hecke-spectral computation (Hejhal, Strömbergsson, KSW).
