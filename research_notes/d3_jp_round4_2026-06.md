# D3 round-4 — Jenkinson–Pollicott determinant attack on C_q (2026-06)

Goal: resolve the large-q verdict on the Hensley-type constant
`C_q ?= 6/(π²·λ_q)` that round-3 left **MODIFIED-FORM / precision-limited**
(confirmed q=3,4,7; borderline q=5,6,8; refuted q≥9 with the round-3 collocation
operator at N=80, where eigenvalues for B≥32 were unreliable).

## Method
Jenkinson–Pollicott Fredholm-determinant / cycle-expansion method (periodic-orbit
traces of the Rosen-λ_q transfer operator), at extended precision (mpmath dps=50),
replacing the N=80 collocation eigensolve. Code:
- `code/d3_jp_dimension.py` — core JP determinant + dimension extraction.
- `code/d3_jp_smallB_worker.py` — B≤8, q=3..11.
- `code/d3_jp_largeB_worker.py` — B=6,8,10, n_max=4, q=12..20 (ran on M2).
- `code/d3_jp_fleet_worker.py` — fleet dispatch wrapper.

## Outcome — INCONCLUSIVE at large q (not converged)
- **Small q confirmed:** JP recovers `dim E_{≤B}` to the validated anchors
  (q=3 B=2 → 0.5312805062772, matches transfer-operator to ~1e-15). Small-q
  `C_q ≈ 6/(π²λ_q)` holds, consistent with round-3.
- **Large q not in asymptotic regime:** for q≥15 the diagnostic `y = B·(1−D)` is
  still *increasing* at B=4, i.e. pre-asymptotic — the B→∞ limit that defines C_q
  is not yet reached at the B reachable here. Extending to B=6,8,10 did not flatten it.
- **JP vs collocation disagree** at large q (B≤8 window): the two estimators do not
  agree to the precision needed to confirm/refute the `6/(π²λ_q)` form, and neither
  is self-converged across B. → no definitive large-q verdict.

## Verdict
Round-3 conclusion **stands unchanged**: `C_q = 6/(π²λ_q)` is a MODIFIED-FORM law,
not universal; the coefficient drifts downward as q grows. Round-4 did **not**
upgrade this to a clean refutation/confirmation at large q — the regime is
genuinely delicate (pre-asymptotic in B, estimator disagreement). A definitive
large-q verdict needs **larger B with higher precision** (deeper cycle expansion,
dps≫50) than this round ran.

## Status for the breakthrough goals
D3 / `C_q` is a **side diagnostic only** — explicitly NOT load-bearing for Goal 1
(cluster≤2) or Goal 1.5 (uniform all-q). The cluster bound is a `<`-inequality on
the return map (finite geometry), independent of C_q's exact value. This note
closes D3 r4 as "inconclusive, parked"; not on the critical path.
