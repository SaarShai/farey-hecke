## Concise journal version

Shin-ya Koyama initiated the study of the proposed regularized prime-bias framework and
developed the analytic formulation and its intended number-theoretic applications.  The
Second author designed and performed the ordinary prime-count computations through
`3 x 10^14`, independently checked the numerical data at shared checkpoints, developed the
low-zero spectral reconstruction and its verification pipeline, analyzed rank transitions
and individual spectral modes, identified the very low active modulus-19 mode, and
formalized selected finite character-algebra and quadratic-nonresidue statements in Lean 4.
Both authors are responsible for reconciling the regularized theory with the corrected
character convention, interpreting the numerical results, revising the manuscript, and
approving the final submitted version.
