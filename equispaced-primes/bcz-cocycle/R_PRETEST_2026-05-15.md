## 2. Method

- `|c_L|` for `L = 1 … L_max`, `L_max = 400`, at `Q = 500, 1000, 2000, 4000`,
  truncations `M = 4, 8, 16`, twists `m = 0, 1, 3`.
- Exponent `α` in `|c_L| ~ C L^{−α}` by OLS of `log|c_L|` on `log L` over
  `[8, 400]`, with slope std-error and `R²`; plus multi-window robustness
  `[ADV]` and a direct `Σ_L|c_L|` partial-sum convergence test `[SUM]`.
- Q-uniformity is the crux: a genuine summable estimate needs `α ≥ 1+η`
  **stably across Q** (cf. R3's strikingly Q-stable `c_0(M)`).
- Cost control: the autocorrelation is a stationary suspension time-average;
  a centered contiguous 800k-node orbit segment is used for large Q. **`[X1]`
  cross-checks segment vs FULL orbit**: agreement to Δα ≤ 0.03 at Q=2000,
  ≤ 0.06 at Q=3000 — faithful (and well within the gap to the α=1 threshold).

## 4. Results — exponent fits (`[NUMERICAL-ONLY]`)

### 4.1 DECISIVE diagnostic — `normed` (Pearson) exponent, M=8, window [8,400]

```
[QST]  Q      α (bare)      α (m=1)       α (m=3)
       500   0.082±0.061   0.082±0.061   0.083±0.061
      1000   0.454±0.072   0.454±0.072   0.454±0.072
      2000   0.565±0.062   0.565±0.062   0.565±0.062
      4000   0.643±0.077   0.643±0.077   0.643±0.077
```

- **α ≈ 0.08–0.64, far below 1.** Drifts upward with Q but is **not
  Q-stable** and shows **no sign of crossing 1** (it is decelerating:
  +0.37, +0.11, +0.08 per Q-doubling). Best honest reading: a slowly
  converging `α ≈ ½` (consistent with the locked Strömbergsson exponent),
  certainly `α ≤ ~0.65 ≪ 1+η`.
- **The twist is numerically inert.** α(m=0) = α(m=1) = α(m=3) to three
  decimals at *every* Q. The phase `cos(2πm(f_{j+L}−f_j))` ≈ 1 because
  consecutive Farey nodes differ by O(1/Q²); even at lag 400 the
  displacement is ≪ 1. The hypothesised twisted decay upgrade does not
  occur for this cocycle at any tested scale.

### 4.2 `none` (bare Strömbergsson control) and M-robustness

Identical α to `normed` (Pearson differs only by the constant `c_0`):
M=4 α≈0.38→0.81, M=8 α≈0.08→0.64, M=16 α≈−0.04→0.48 over Q=500→4000.
**Larger truncation M gives SMALLER α** (slower decay) — the opposite of
what summability needs as `M→∞` (the (R-input) limit direction). This is an
additional adverse signal: the `M→∞` tail required by (R-structure) makes
the decay *worse*, not summable.

The tail exponent — the one summability depends on — is never ≥ 1.

### 4.4 `single`-roof weight (literal §3 object) — apparent α≈1 is roof-tail artifact

`single` (`w=R_j`) reaches α≈1.0–1.2 at M≥8, Q≥2000. But: (a) it is
**not Q-stable** (M=8: 0.33→0.73→1.04→1.05; M=16: 0.56→0.99→1.19→1.16 —
plateauing *below or near* 1, not robustly above 1+η); (b) the underlying
`normed` decorrelation is α≈0.5 (§4.1); the apparent extra decay is the
heavy-tailed roof `R` (Hall `L^p`, `p<2`) re-weighting toward small-gap
nodes, **not** cocycle mixing. The `sqrt` weight (also a legitimate reading)
gives **negative α** (correlation *grows*: α≈−0.1 to −0.4, R²≈0.4) — pure
roof-product variance injection. The roof-weight choice swings α from −0.4
to +1.2: the "summability" is an artifact of which heavy-tailed weight one
picks, not a property of the cocycle. The roof-tail-free `normed` reading
(α≈½) is the honest one.

### 4.5 Direct Σ|c_L| convergence `[SUM]` (decisive, normed, M=8)

```
Q=2000: PS@[25,50,100,200,400]=[1.118,1.265,1.475,1.696,2.033]
        doubling incs PS[2L]−PS[L] = [0.147, 0.210, 0.220, 0.337]

The doubling increments **increase** monotonically. A summable series has
geometrically *shrinking* doubling increments; a non-summable `~L^{−α}`,
`α<1`, series has increments `~ L^{1−α}` that *grow*. Observed behaviour is
unambiguously the **non-summable** signature. No partial-sum stabilization
at any Q.
