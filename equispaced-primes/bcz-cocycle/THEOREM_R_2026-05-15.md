### 0.2 Strömbergsson — effective horocycle equidistribution — `[PROVEN(literature, verified location)]`

> f ∈ C⁴(Γ\G) such that ‖f‖_{W⁴} < ∞ and ‖f‖_N < ∞:
> (1/T) ∫₀ᵀ f(h_t(p)) dt = ⟨f⟩ + O( ‖f‖_{W⁴} { r^{−½} log³(r+2) +

**Specialization to Γ = PSL(2,ℤ) (the modular surface, our X).** The modular

`r = T·e^{−dist(...)} → ∞`, and `dist` is bounded on the relevant compact
excursion since the Farey horocycle base point `(1/Q,1)` projects to a fixed
compact part). **Net: a square-root power saving `≪ T^{−1/2+ε}` in the
horocycle ergodic average.**

## 1. Exact starting identity (PROVEN, in-repo V3/V4)

Let `Q ∈ ℕ`, `Φ = |F_Q|`, the ordered nodes `0 = f_0 < f_1 < ⋯ < f_Φ = 1`
(`f_0 = 0` boundary convention, V4). `E_Q(x) = #{f ≤ x} − Φx`, and
`S_j := E_Q(f_j) = j − Φ f_j` (exact, V4). The Hall-normalized cocycle is
`ψ_j := 1 − s_j` with `s_j := Φ·gap_j`, `gap_j = f_j − f_{j−1}`. Then
`S_j = Σ_{i=1}^{j} ψ_i` is the Birkhoff sum of `ψ` along the BCZ orbit, and
`S_0 = S_Φ = 0` (**closed orbit**, telescoping). Quadrature gives the
**exact identity** (V3, PROVEN):

> `J(Q) = ∫₀¹ E_Q² = Σ_{j} gap_j ( S_j² − S_j Φ gap_j + (Φ gap_j)²/3 )`,
> leading term `L(Q) := Σ_j gap_j · S_j²`.

`W(Q) = J(Q)/Φ + O(1/Φ)`, target `C = lim_{Q→∞} Q·W(Q) ≈ 0.66`.

`R1` (numerics, exact): `⟨s⟩ = Φ·Σgap_j / Φ = 1` **exactly** (telescoping);
so `ψ` is *exactly* mean-zero, no centering error — a clean improvement over
