Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript "Regularized Spectral Statistics for Prime Races and
Low-Zero Transient Reversals" by Shin-ya Koyama and Saar Shai for
consideration in Experimental Mathematics.

The paper reports verified finite computations on prime races modulo
N in {7, 8, 11, 19, 23}: ordinary prime-count data at 438 checkpoints through
3×10^14, agreeing exactly with an independently checked baseline in all 567
shared cells through 1.3×10^13; low-zero explicit-formula reconstructions
that track the observed transient trajectories at correlations 0.83–0.99;
and an interval-arithmetic certification of a previously unnoticed
critical-line zero of ultra-low height (γ ≈ 0.0190) for the odd character of
Conrey index 13 modulo 19. Selected finite character-algebra statements are
formalized in Lean 4. These computations motivate, and sharply delimit, a
conjectural Gaussian-regularized fine-structure hierarchy whose theoretical
framework appears in the companion preprint arXiv:2607.28931 by the first
author; the manuscript keeps a strict boundary between proved identities,
verified computation, and conjecture.

The complete data and code package is permanently archived at Zenodo
(DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX).

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. Both authors approve
the submission.

Sincerely,
Shin-ya Koyama and Saar Shai
