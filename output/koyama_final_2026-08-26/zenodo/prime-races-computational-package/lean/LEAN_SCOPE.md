# Lean scope

The packet includes two small source projects:

- `character-selector/CharacterOrthogonalityTarget.lean` formalizes the
  corrected finite character-selector identity and an inverse-class witness.
- `nonresidue-leading-mean/Minus1Core.lean` contains four finite statements
  about the leading quadratic-nonresidue mean.

The listed `.lean` files contain no `sorry` or `admit`. Their toolchain and
dependency manifests are included, but build caches are intentionally omitted.

Lean does **not** formalize the two-parameter regularized limit, explicit-formula
estimates, numerical computations, DRH/GRH, or the manuscript as a whole.
