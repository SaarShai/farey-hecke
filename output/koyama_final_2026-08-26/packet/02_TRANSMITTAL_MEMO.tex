\section*{Main numerical findings}

The ordinary-count file has 438 checkpoints for
$N\in\{7,8,11,19,23\}$ and agrees exactly with the independently checked
baseline in all 567 shared cells through $1.3\times10^{13}$. The continuation
to $3\times10^{14}$ has now been replicated by an independent
primesieve-based implementation on Kaggle: 4896/4896 shared cells match
exactly at all 72 frontier grid points, and the range counts reproduce
the published values of $\pi(10^{14})$, $\pi(2\times10^{14})$, and
$\pi(3\times10^{14})$ exactly (see \texttt{numerics/frontier\_replication/}
in the computational package).

Using 25 positive zeros per nonprincipal character, the top-decade
correlations for the normalized $-1$ trajectories are $0.965$, $0.931$,
$0.939$, $0.971$, and $0.826$. At the final checkpoint, the ranks of $-1$ are
$1/3$, $3/3$, $3/5$, $6/9$, and $1/11$, respectively. These continuing
reversals motivate regularization but do not test the conjectural hierarchy.

For the odd character of Conrey index 13 modulo 19, an independent Arb
sign-change computation certifies a critical-line zero near
$\gamma=0.018956399080226143$. With 100 positive ordinates per nonprincipal
character modulo 19, the $-1$ correlation is $0.9925$, while rank and leader
changes remain active. The earlier $e^{33.4}$ settling estimate is therefore
not retained.

\section*{Why the requested mollified plot is pending}

The current analytic argument does not yet fix a unique finite statistic,
normalization, logarithm branch, or admissible regime $T=T(x)$. It also lacks a
summed off-diagonal estimate. A comparison against
$C_{N,T}\,B_N(a)$ before those choices are fixed could be rescaled after the
fact. The revision therefore states the two-parameter limit honestly as a
conjectural target and supplies the verified ordinary-count reconstruction
instead.
