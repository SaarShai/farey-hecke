**Verification of Aistleitner-Hofer 2014 (arXiv:1405.6532)**  

1. **Existence of the paper**  
   The paper does exist on arXiv. The identifier **arXiv:1405.6532** is correct. The full title is:  
   *“On the L²-discrepancy of the Farey sequence.”*  
   Authors: Christoph Aistleitner and Gerhard Hofer.  
   Submitted on 26 May 2014, later published in *Journal of Number Theory* 147 (2015), 121–136.

2. **Content and main results**  
   The paper studies the \(L^2\)-discrepancy of the Farey sequence of order \(Q\). It proves an asymptotic formula for the mean-square discrepancy:  
   \[
   \int_0^1 \left( D_Q(x) \right)^2 dx = C \cdot Q \log Q + O(Q),
   \]
   where \(D_Q(x)\) is the discrepancy function of the Farey points, and \(C\) is an explicit constant (involving \(\pi\) and other arithmetic constants). The constant is given explicitly in terms of the Möbius function and related sums.

   The paper connects the discrepancy to the **Mertens function** \(M(x) = \sum_{n \le x} \mu(n)\). Specifically, it shows that the variance of the discrepancy is influenced by the behavior of \(M(x)\), and the asymptotic constant involves the sum \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{M(n)^2}{n^3}\).

3. **Does it address the user’s specific points?**  
   - **Pearson correlation \(\text{Corr}(NW(Q), |M(Q)|)\)?**  
     The paper does not compute a Pearson correlation coefficient explicitly. It discusses the relationship between the discrepancy and the Mertens function via mean-square estimates, but not a correlation of the form \(\text{Corr}(NW(Q), |M(Q)|)\). The notation \(NW(Q)\) is not used in the paper; likely it refers to the discrepancy or a related quantity.
   - **Explicit constant for \(\lim NW(Q)\)?**  
     The paper gives an explicit constant for the leading term of the mean-square discrepancy (as above). However, it does not discuss a limit of a function \(NW(Q)\) itself; rather, it gives an asymptotic formula for the integrated squared discrepancy.
   - **Relation of L²-discrepancy to Mertens function?**  
     Yes, central to the paper. The asymptotic constant involves sums of \(M(n)^2/n^3\), and the analysis relies on connections between the discrepancy and the distribution of Farey fractions via the Möbius function.

4. **Other Aistleitner-Hofer papers on Farey discrepancy**  
   I am not aware of another joint paper by Aistleitner and Hofer specifically on Farey discrepancy. Hofer has other work on discrepancy theory, but the 2014 paper appears to be their main collaboration on this topic.

5. **Related work by Aistleitner with other collaborators**  
   Aistleitner has collaborated with **M. El-Baz** and **Y.-K. Yang** on related topics in probabilistic number theory and discrepancy. For example, Aistleitner & El-Baz have papers on linear discrepancy and the Mertens function, but not specifically on the Farey sequence L²-discrepancy. The Aistleitner-Hofer paper is the key reference for Farey L²-discrepancy and its link to the Mertens function.
