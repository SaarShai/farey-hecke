# N18 — Farey gaps and quantum chaos

## Setup

Our N10 finding: Farey gaps' lag-1 correlation = +1/2 (level ATTRACTION), placing it OUTSIDE standard random-matrix universality classes (GOE/GUE/GSE all have lag-1 ≤ 0, level REPULSION).

Recent connection (Marklof-Strömbergsson): the Farey sequence corresponds to the SL(2,Z) flow on SL(2,R)/SL(2,Z), and its gap distribution is related to:
- 3-distance theorem (Steinhaus)
- BCZ joint density (mentioned)
- Quantum unique ergodicity (QUE) — Lindenstrauss

## Question

Does the cluster-2 / lag-1=+1/2 structure have a QUANTUM-MECHANICAL analog?

Specifically:
- The Berry-Tabor conjecture says spectral statistics of INTEGRABLE quantum systems are Poisson (uncorrelated gaps).
- BGS conjecture: spectral statistics of generically CHAOTIC systems are GOE/GUE.
- Farey is INTERMEDIATE — neither Poisson nor RMT.

Is there a known integrable system whose energy levels have:
- Lag-1 correlation = +1/2 (deterministic level attraction)
- Cluster-size-2 extremes

If so, that's a quantum analog of the Farey structure. If not, Farey gives a NEW universality class for some intermediate spectral statistics regime.
