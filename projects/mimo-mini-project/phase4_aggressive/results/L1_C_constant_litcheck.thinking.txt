### **1. Franel–Landau (1924)**  
Franel and Landau both considered the discrepancy of Farey sequences. Franel (1924) gave the formula:  
\[

\]  
where \( D(n) \) is the Farey discrepancy, but this is for the cumulative discrepancy up to \( N \), not the \( L^2 \)-norm of \( E_N(x) \).  
Landau (1924) improved the bounds but did not explicitly evaluate the constant.  

**Verdict:** Neither Franel nor Landau explicitly compute \( C \) as defined in your \( J(N)/|F_N| \) problem. They derive an asymptotic for the squared discrepancy sum, but not in the form \( N \cdot W(N) \to C \).

### **2. Mikolás (1949)**  
Mikolás (1949) gave the formula:  
\[

where \( A_N(m) \) is a sum over Farey fractions with denominator dividing \( m \).  
He then studied asymptotics, but I don’t recall him extracting the exact limit constant. The formula is often used later, e.g., by Codecá–Perelli.  

**Verdict:** Mikolás gives the series representation, but not the explicit value of \( C \).
