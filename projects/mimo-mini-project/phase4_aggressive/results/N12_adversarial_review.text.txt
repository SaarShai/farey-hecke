### **Claim 3: L-zero tomography via MUSIC works for function fields, Dirichlet L, modular form L**

**Strongest counter-argument:**  
MUSIC is a high-resolution spectral estimation method; its success depends on the signal being well-approximated by a sum of sinusoids. The zeros of L-functions have subtle correlations (e.g., Montgomery’s pair correlation) that may not fit this model exactly. Incomplete literature check: there may be prior work applying MUSIC or similar methods to L-functions in inverse problems or quantum chaos.

**Killing experiment:**  
Apply MUSIC to a known L-function with a computed zero set (e.g., Riemann zeta up to height 10⁶) and compare with direct zero computation. If MUSIC fails to recover zeros with high accuracy, the method is flawed.

**Honest assessment:**  
**High potential for survival** if the method works empirically and is novel. But must verify that MUSIC’s assumptions hold for the specific structure of L-function zeros. Survival probability: **medium-high** if validated.
