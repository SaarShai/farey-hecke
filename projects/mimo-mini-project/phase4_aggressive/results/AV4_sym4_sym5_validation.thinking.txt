### Using the explicit formula to estimate

The explicit formula for ψ_Sym^k(x) = Σ_{p≤x} λ_p(Sym^k Δ) log(p) involves:

ψ(x) ≈ -Σ_ρ x^ρ/ρ + (smooth)

where ρ = 1/2 + iγ are the nontrivial zeros.

MUSIC is trying to decompose ψ(x) (or rather ψ evaluated at log-spaced points) into a sum of terms x^ρ = e^{(1/2+iγ)log(x)} = x^{1/2} e^{iγ log(x)}.

After dividing by x^{1/2}, the oscillatory signal is:

e^{-iγ₁ t} + e^{-iγ₂ t} + ... where t = log(x)

MUSIC identifies these frequencies.
