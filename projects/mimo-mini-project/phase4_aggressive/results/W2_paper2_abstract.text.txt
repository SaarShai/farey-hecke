**Title:** Positive Correlation and Deterministic Clustering in Farey Sequence Gaps: A New Universality Class Beyond Wigner-Dyson

**Keywords:** Farey sequence, gap distribution, level spacing correlation, extreme value theory, dynamical recurrence

**Abstract:**
We present statistical evidence that the gaps between consecutive fractions in the Farey sequence \(F_N\) form a novel random matrix-like system, yet one that fundamentally deviates from all standard Wigner-Dyson universality classes. Empirical analysis up to \(N=50{,}000\) reveals that consecutive gap lengths exhibit a lag-1 Pearson correlation converging to \(+1/2\) as \(N\to\infty\). This strong positive correlation is driven by a deterministic dynamical mechanism: the mediant recurrence of the Farey construction forces large gaps to appear in precisely paired clusters of size two. Consequently, for extreme gaps, the extremal index \(\theta = 1/2\) emerges exactly, with 99.99% of high-threshold clusters being deterministically of size 2. This "BCZ pair" mechanism (derived from the Farey tree recurrence) also explains the observed scaling ratio \(\mathcal{D}^*(F'_N)/\mathcal{D}^*(F_N) \to 1/2\) for the prime sequence. Our central finding is that this \(+1/2\) correlation contradicts the non-positive correlations universal in classical ensembles (GOE, Poisson, etc.). The Farey gaps thus define a distinct, number-theoretic universality class characterized by positive correlation and deterministic clustering. This suggests deep connections to symbolic dynamics that merit rigorous investigation.
