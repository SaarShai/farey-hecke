If NW(Q) measures the discrepancy of the Farey sequence or something similar, then spikes might correspond to Q values where the discrepancy is anomalously high.

The discrepancy of the Farey sequence F_Q is known to be related to the Riemann Hypothesis. Under RH, the discrepancy is O(log Q / Q), but without RH, it could be larger.

Specifically, the discrepancy D(Q) of the Farey sequence F_Q is:
D(Q) = max |A(I) / |F_Q| - |I|| 
where A(I) is the number of Farey fractions in interval I.

The maximum discrepancy over all intervals I is related to the distribution of Farey fractions, which depends on the Möbius function.
