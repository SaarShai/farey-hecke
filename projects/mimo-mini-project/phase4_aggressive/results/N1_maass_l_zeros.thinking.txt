Now, let me think about the MUSIC algorithm itself.

MUSIC (MUltiple SIgnal Classification) works as follows:
1. Given a signal x[n] = Σ_k A_k e^{iω_k n} + noise
2. Form the autocorrelation matrix R from the data
3. Eigendecompose R into signal and noise subspaces
4. The MUSIC pseudospectrum P(ω) = 1 / ||e(ω)^H · U_n||² (where U_n is the noise subspace)
5. Peaks in P(ω) give estimates of the frequencies ω_k

For our application:
- The "signal" is Δ_E(x_n) for log-spaced x_n
- The "frequencies" are the γ values (imaginary parts of zeros)
- The "samples" are at t_n = log(x_n), equally spaced
