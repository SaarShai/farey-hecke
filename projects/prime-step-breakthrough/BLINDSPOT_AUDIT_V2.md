# Multidimensional Operational Blindspot Audit

Date: 2026-07-15

## What good looks like

The release is successful only if the mathematical comparison set, the emitted
order, and the verified software path agree.  A useful result must emit one
million positions within the frozen wall/RSS budget, support a precisely stated
constraint language, independently recompute feasibility and discrepancy, and
attach a lower bound that is valid for the same true optimum.  A domain analogy
is not an application result; a theorem of existence is not an implemented
runtime; a passing unit test is not a live browser result.

## Unknown unknowns converted into tests

1. **Does a plausible fair scheduler actually satisfy quota?** No for naive
   deficit and virtual-finish rules.  Release/deadline EDF must be checked
   against independent quota reachability and exact optimum DPs.
2. **Does one DP label per subset preserve a lexicographic objective?** No.  A
   cold seven-item counterexample forces the two-pass peak-then-sum oracle.
3. **Does a constructive Steinitz citation imply linear time?** No.  Kadec is
   `O(N^d)` and Bárány is quadratic; both fail the million-item requirement.
4. **Do constraints preserve the free factor?** No.  A sparse DAG forcing all
   positive scalar vectors before all negative ones makes the loss linear.
5. **Can block totals hide damage?** Yes.  A zero-total block can contain an
   arbitrarily large internal excursion; every item-level prefix must be checked.
6. **Can coordinate integrality be called the optimum?** No.  It is a lower
   bound and may be weak; the result must display the actual ratio or no ratio.
7. **Does finite joint-cell balance imply star discrepancy or accurate tails?**
   No.  Within-cell geometry, rare boundaries, interactions, and unmeasured
   variables remain uncontrolled.
8. **Does better ordering help if every item is always processed?** Not the final
   answer.  Value requires interruption, progressive monitoring, checkpointing,
   or an externally valid stopping rule.
9. **Can a million-item order be returned as synchronous JSON?** Not safely.
   Compact counts plus digest/preview or a controlled artifact are required.
10. **Can exact arithmetic be delegated to browser numbers?** No.  The server
    owns exact integer products; browser display uses canonical strings.

## Historical work that bounds the claim

- Balinski and Young establish house-monotone quota paths; the release's
  release/deadline EDF formulation, fast heap implementation, and approximation
  certificate are a synthesis requiring its own proof and verification.
- Christoffel/mechanical words solve the binary structure; they do not supply a
  constructor for three or more categories.
- Grinberg-Sevastyanov proves the free Steinitz existence bound; Kadec, Bárány,
  and newer constructive work do not justify the frozen million-scale general
  vector claim.
- Fair queueing and deficit round robin are serious operational baselines but
  do not imply exact two-sided quota.
- Progressive sampling, Monte Carlo/QMC, and covariate balancing provide domain
  motivations and incumbent baselines, not automatic value evidence.

## Potholes the implementation must prevent

- ratio division when `OPT=0` or the lower bound is zero;
- terminal-prefix convention drift;
- duplicate IDs being confused with duplicate vectors;
- float/nonfinite input receiving an exact guarantee;
- blocks split by pins or internal precedence reversed;
- a cycle or empty ready frontier mislabeled as a proof of infeasibility outside
  the restricted V1 language;
- weakened performance thresholds or forged `passed` artifact fields;
- source mutation, cache files, arbitrary file paths, oversized request bodies,
  negative content lengths, or unbounded result JSON;
- application text claiming speed, savings, risk accuracy, clinical validity,
  or production readiness without measured integration evidence.

## Remaining research questions

- Is there a simple practical constructor for the general `L-infinity`
  Steinitz problem with a useful dimension-only factor?
- Which restricted precedence classes retain a nontrivial approximation?
- Can stronger dual/checkpoint lower bounds remain independently verifiable at
  million scale?
- Which feature dictionaries give certified approximations to full geometric
  discrepancy without an exponential number of cells?
- Do real renderers or scenario engines reach a validated stopping threshold
  earlier after quota ordering than after native progressive sequences?
