## Historical result-status corrections

- The static Farey--Mertens bridge and Ramanujan-sum evaluation are prior art.
- The natural \(M(p)<0\) sign rule is false at \(p=92173\).
- The stronger repository sign target has an archived full-increment
  counterexample at \(p=243799\) that still needs a fresh convention-matched
  reproduction; it is excluded from this architecture either way.
- Positivity of the cross term \(B\) is false at \(p=237733\) and \(243799\).
- A fitted limit for \(N W(N)\) is not a proved limit; old constants conflict.
- The raw BCZ cocycle has the wrong uniform integrability/correlation behaviour
  for the naive CLT route.
- One-dimensional Farey quadrature has favourable examples and severe
  resonant failures; current multidimensional versions lose badly to standard
  low-discrepancy baselines.
- Later Hecke work is a separate descendant and cannot be used to inflate the
  status of the original prime-step claims.
- The fixed \(2\ldots200\), exactly-ten-layer optimizer loses to a same-point
  midpoint rule and to broader admissible prime/divisor grid choices.  Its
  practical claim is restricted to the literal cap and exact layer count.

## Publication boundary

The strongest honest phrase before external peer review is:

> We prove, within this project and subject to independent proof review, a
> quantitative triangular limit law for the prime-step Farey shift that
> resolves a documented conjecture in the repository.  A focused primary-source
> search found no exact moving-multiplier statement, but this is not a claim of
> exhaustive external novelty.  The layer-kernel application is an exact new
> synthesis unless and until a formula-level predecessor is identified.
